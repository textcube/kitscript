How to Play

Fly the neon jet through six stages. Your cannon fires automatically. Bomb ground targets, refuel from NRG tanks, and survive the bosses.

Arrows / WASDSteer Space / X / ZDrop bomb P / EscPause TouchDrag to steer, tap or BOMB button to bomb

FUEL drains constantly. Shoot or bomb NRG tanks (or touch floating pods) to refill. Run dry and you crash.

BOMBS arc forward and down: only bombs and blasts reach ground targets. CHECKPOINTS save your place mid-stage. Grab S shield and W spread capsules.
